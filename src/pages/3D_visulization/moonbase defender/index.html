📜 Story

The year is 2147. Humanity's last stronghold on the moon, Moonbase Sigma, was built to harness the mysterious energy of ancient alien crystals buried deep beneath the lunar surface.

These crystals are the key to Earth’s survival a limitless power source capable of reversing climate collapse and powering future generations.

But the excavation awoke something ancient. A hostile alien species — long dormant beneath the dust — has returned. They see the crystals as sacred, and humanity as a threat.

One by one, communication with other bases has gone silent. You are the last defender of Moonbase Sigma. Your mission is critical: defend the crystals, repel the invaders, and escape with the final evacuation ship once all power cores are activated.

Armed with lunar weapons and experimental tech, you must survive wave after wave of enemies in the cratered shadows of the moon.

This is your last stand, soldier. Suit up.
